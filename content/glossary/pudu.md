**Pudu** (普度) means "universal salvation" or "ferrying across." It refers to rites that offer food and prayers to wandering and hungry spirits, with the aim of relieving their suffering and guiding them toward release.

Pudu rites are most prominent during Ghost Month and at the [[hungry-ghost-festival|Zhongyuan Festival]].
