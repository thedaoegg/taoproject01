The Zhongyuan Festival falls on the 15th day of the 7th lunar month. In Taoism it is the birthday of the Earthly Official (地官, *Diguan*), one of the Three Officials, who pardons sins.

It is also the peak of Ghost Month, when popular belief holds that the gates of the underworld open and spirits wander the human world. Priests perform [[pudu]] rites to feed and liberate these hungry ghosts, and families make offerings for their ancestors.

## Customs

- Offerings of food, incense and paper goods
- Rites for the deceased performed by priests
- Avoiding risky ventures during the month, in folk custom
