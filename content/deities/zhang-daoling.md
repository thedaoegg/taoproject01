Zhang Daoling (traditionally dated 34–156 CE) is honored as the first **Celestial Master** (天師, *Tianshi*) and the founder of the Way of the Celestial Masters, the movement from which the Zhengyi tradition descends.

According to tradition, in 142 CE Taishang Laojun, the deified Laozi, appeared to him and appointed him his representative on earth. The covenant he received is the foundation of Zhengyi ritual and priestly ordination.

His descendants inherited the title of Celestial Master, and the lineage is closely associated with Longhu Mountain (龍虎山).

## Observance

His birthday is traditionally kept on the 18th day of the 5th lunar month. Some sources give other dates, so check with your own temple or teacher.
